Established in 2009 and incorporated in 2010, our mission is to bring hope to our city through direct service programs. We have ten (10) programs focusing on education, food, and health. We strive to mitigate the effects of food scarcity and food insecurity. Each program was designed to answer a need in our city. Programs include English & Citizenship lessons, a Super Food Pantry & Baby Center, the “Feel the Warmth” weekly meal, the first non-profit Indoor Hydroponic Garden in the state, and more. nOURish BRIDGEPORT, Inc. is also known for building partnerships across Bridgeport, Fairfield County, and Connecticut. We view these collaborative efforts as ways to connect folks with means with folks with needs, and to give all groups and families opportunities to serve others. We seek to follow the philosophy that we all need to work together for the common good. We welcome over 2,500 volunteers each year. In 2020, nOURish was honored as the “Non-Profit Partner of the Year” by the Center for Family Justice, and President/CEO and Founder, Reverend Sara D. Smith, Esq. – on behalf of nOURish, has been awarded a number of other honors for nOURish’s work with refugees, women’s leadership, volunteers and more. nOURish Bridgeport was the location and mission partner host to our Youth Fellowship Mission Team in June 2024, where our young people were able to participate in many important projects and learning.
Undue Medical Debt (formerly RIP Medical Debt) is a national 501(c)(3) nonprofit whose purpose is to strengthen communities by erasing financially burdensome medical debt. Founded in 2014 by former debt collection executives, Undue Medical Debt is one of the leading charitable organizations that help pay medical bills. We work tirelessly to end medical debt and ensure a future where everyone can access healthcare without fear. UMD’s unique method uses donations to buy medical debt in large, bundled portfolios, pinpointing the debt of those most in need. Able to buy the debt at a steep discount, UMD ensures that each $1 donation erases about $100 of medical debt. Through our partnership with UMD over the years both through Yankee Fair mission proceeds and our own budgeted mission funds, the Congregational Church of Brookfield has helped to erase over $1million in medical debt for those in the tri-state area and beyond.
* Note: 25% of our Yankee Fair Funds will be divided among these 2 mission partners. 25% goes to our ongoing programs and ministries. 25% goes to our Building & Grounds Fund. The other 25% goes to the Christian Growth & Enrichment Fund, which is used to fund church members’ participation in wider church meetings, mission trips, retreats, music festivals, summer camps, educational opportunities and more.
