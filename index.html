Grounds Improvement
The Congregational Church of Brookfield celebrated a ground-breaking ceremony for a new fully accessible addition to our Fellowship Hall last month.

As an historic church in the center of Brookfield, we have been a familiar and cherished landmark serving the community for over 250 years. Our congregation recognized the need to make our facilities more accessible to those living with disabilities, including those who are unable to navigate stairs, to better fulfill our mission of being a congregation where all are welcome.

We have begun a major addition to install four new ADA bathrooms and an elevator to make all three levels- the Sanctuary, Church School wing and Fellowship Hall- accessible. Other improvements to the space are also in progress to update the appearance, lighting and technology of the space. This will benefit our members, as well as our local community and others. The goal is to allow all to fully participate in our church services, and other events, and have access to the resources that our church provides, regardless of any physical limitations. Our space is used and available for many outside groups to hold various functions such as meetings, workshops, and personal events including parties, celebrations, showers, presentations, dinners and luncheons. The building improvements will improve these experiences.

We feel so grateful to those who give and continue to give financial support to make our Fellowship Hall accessible as well as the other improvements.
